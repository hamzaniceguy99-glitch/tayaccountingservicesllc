How we work

A Florida company, and not a CPA firm

TAY Accounting Services LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L24000130826, filed in 2024, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We keep books. We are not a certified public accounting firm, we do not perform audits, reviews or compilations, and nothing we prepare carries assurance — so it should never be handed to a lender as reviewed or audited. Tax returns and representation before a taxing authority go to a CPA or enrolled agent, and we will tell you when you have reached that point.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you file my tax return?

No. We prepare the records your CPA or enrolled agent files from, and we will refer you if you do not have one. Keeping that line clear is to your benefit.

My books are two years behind. Is that hopeless?

No, and it is very common. Catch-up is quoted separately after we have looked, because anyone who prices it sight unseen is guessing.

What does the monthly fee include?

Stated in writing before we start. Catch-up work and one-off projects are always separate, so the monthly figure stays predictable.

Can you give my bank reviewed financials?

No. That is an attest service and requires a CPA firm. We can prepare the underlying records for a CPA to work from.

Which software do you use?

Whatever you already use, within reason. If you are on spreadsheets we will tell you honestly whether that is still working.

Do you handle sales tax?

Yes, including watching your assigned filing frequency, which the state changes more often than people expect.

Behind on the books?

A free review, and an honest quote for catching up. No obligation.

How we work

Close it monthly, query it early

Books reconstructed once a year are a historical record. Books closed monthly are a management tool — you can see margin slipping in month three rather than discovering it the following spring when nothing can be done about it.

Closing monthly also makes the work accurate. A transaction queried three weeks after it happened gets answered correctly; the same question asked eleven months later gets a guess, and guesses are what make year end expensive.

The other thing worth being plain about is scope. Bookkeeping is not attest work, and records we prepare carry no assurance. Handing a lender something labelled as reviewed when it was not is a problem for the business owner, not the bookkeeper, so we say it clearly.
